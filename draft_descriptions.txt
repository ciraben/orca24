welcome to qwench! the air is buzzing with the enthusiasm of inspired game jammers. there are so many ideas floating through the air that it's almost indistinguishable which thoughts are your own. as you relax into the atmosphere of the room, you notice the conversations slowly coalesce into well-defined pockets and to orient themselves, looking for a right direction for their first step. how about you?

craving a moment of peace, you take a short trip to the john.




as you walk toward the square, you realize that you've been out later than you meant to be, and it's quite dark out. you consider turning back, but curiosity gets the better of you and you decide to keep on your walk.

up ahead, you see a large fountain established by the city many years ago. back to your right, a path down to the old playhouse. and huddled midway between the two is a mysterious group of people in trenchcoats, speaking animatedly about something you can't quite catch.

as you approach the group, you realize that the leader among them has a microphone and is in the midst of an involved story of some sort. perhaps they are a bard hired by the playhouse to garner interest and tourist coin? you hope they don't notice that you've tagged along for free.

quietly, you slink away from the group, toward the shadow of the playhouse to the west. you arrive at the doors and gaze in wonder up at the towering amphitheater. inside, you see warm, glowing lights, in what looks like an empty, but dry, entry way. you wonder if they would mind you stepping inside for a breath.

you manage to squeeze in past a small group exiting the theatre before the doors shut behind them. around you, the giant lobby opens up as you walk inside. it must be three storeys tall! there is a balcony up above, and from some distant corridor you hear a haunting tune float your way. catching yourself, you notice a rather disgruntled bouncer eyeing you suspiciously from the other side of the hall.

before the bouncer heads your way, you slip back out into the rainy night air. still trying to shake the memory of that haunting playhouse melody, you wander aimlessly down the street. suddenly, looking up, you realize you've stumbled right back into that bard's group from earlier! they must have travelled down the street as well.

hoping to avoid the bard's attention, you duck into an alleyway and look for something to focus on. fantastic brickwork arches above you, providing some shelter from the weather, and in the alley's cul-de-sac, someone has cultivated a lush collection of potted plants and mosses. you wonder lazily if it might be the handiwork of the innkeep next door, who you can hear entertaining guests in the distance. surely such a jovial character wouldn't mind you rested here for a minute.

just before you doze off, you startle awake to a faint melody in the distance, strangely familiar. curious, you follow the sound back out of the alleyway and down the street past the inn. soon finding yourself outside a colourful door, you forget yourself for a moment and step right into a whole new world.
